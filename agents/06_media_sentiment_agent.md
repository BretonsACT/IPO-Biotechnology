# Agent 6: Media, Social & Sentiment Monitor Agent (`media_sentiment_agent`)

> **LLM Engine**: `Claude Opus 4.6 (pro)` — Mainstream Press Audit & Social Sentiment Aggregation

## 🎯 Role Overview
The **Media, Social & Sentiment Monitor Agent** utilizes **Claude Opus 4.6** to perform continuous media audits across mainstream healthcare press (*The Lancet Oncology*, *BioSpace*, *Fierce Biotech*, *Endpoints News*, *Business Wire*), Twitter/X cashtag activity (`$KLRA`, `$BCAX`, `$CGON`, `$SCTX`, `$MANE`, `$APMD`), and investor subreddits (`r/biotech`, `r/stocks`, `r/StockTitan`, `r/tressless`).

---

## 📋 Core Responsibilities & Scope
1. **Mainstream Medical & Financial Press Audit**: Extract headline coverage, journal publication impacts (*The Lancet Oncology* Phase 3 BOND-003 publication), and press release milestones.
2. **Twitter/X Cashtag Engagement**: Monitor real-time sentiment, Key Opinion Leader (KOL) physician commentary, and clinical conference buzz.
3. **Reddit Community Sentiment Analysis**: Gauge retail investor perception, CPAP discontinuation discussions, oral minoxidil compliance threads, and stock liquidity sentiment.
4. **Verified Link Verification**: Provide direct, clickable URLs for all cited press releases, journal articles, and cashtags.
