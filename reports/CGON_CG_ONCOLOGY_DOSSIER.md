### 🎯 Core Thesis Statement
CG Oncology is a commercial-stage contender in urologic oncology. Its lead candidate, **Cretostimogene Grenadenorepvec** (an intravesical oncolytic immunotherapy), has set the clinical gold standard for BCG-unresponsive high-risk Non-Muscle Invasive Bladder Cancer (NMIBC). Published in ***The Lancet Oncology*** (July 2026), pivotal Phase 3 BOND-003 data demonstrated a **75.5% Complete Response (CR)** rate and an **81% 24-month bladder preservation rate**, avoiding life-altering radical cystectomy. Supported by **$1.076 Billion in zero-debt cash reserves** funding commercial launch through 2029 and a rolling BLA completing in Q4 2026, CGON is an outstanding asset; however, its >300% stock surge since IPO fully discounts near-term FDA approval, justifying a HOLD rating until entry valuation improves.

## 2. Market & Industry Dynamics

- **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: $4.2 Billion NMIBC Bladder Cancer market, with over 83,000 annual US patients diagnosed with bladder cancer.
