## 3. Granular Bull vs. Bear Investment Case

### 🟢 The Bull Case (Upside Drivers)
1. **Multi-Billion Cash-Pay Market**: Solves a major compliance problem in an $8.5B out-of-pocket dermatology TAM.
2. **Cardiovascular Safety Moat**: Proprietary extended-release formulation eliminates systemic hypotension risks.
3. **Massive Capital Buffer**: $766.8M total capital raised extends cash runway into 2030.
4. **Phase 3 Registration Data**: Positive Study 302 topline data ($p < 0.001$) paves clear FDA registration path.
5. **Female Pattern Hair Loss Expansion**: Active Phase 2/3 Study 306 opens additional consumer market segment.

### 🔴 The Bear Case (Risk Headwinds)
1. **Generic Minoxidil Compounding**: Competition from cheap off-label oral minoxidil compounders and telehealth platforms.
2. **Consumer Cash-Pay Price Sensitivity**: Macroeconomic headwinds impacting out-of-pocket aesthetic spending.
3. **Patent Enforcement Challenges**: Defending extended-release formulation patents against generic litigation.
4. **Commercial Sales Infrastructure**: Requires building out dermatology direct-to-consumer and clinic distribution.
