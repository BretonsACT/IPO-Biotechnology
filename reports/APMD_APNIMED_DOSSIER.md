## 2. In-Depth Clinical Pipeline & Trial Endpoints

## 3. Granular Bull vs. Bear Investment Case

### 🟢 The Bull Case (Upside Drivers)
1. **CPAP Machine Alternative**: First viable oral pill option in a $12B sleep apnea TAM.
2. **80%+ Discontinuation Conversion**: Over 80% of diagnosed OSA patients discontinue CPAP hardware within 1 year, driving massive market adoption.
3. **Phase 3 Statistical Significance**: LunAIRo trial (n=660) demonstrated 46.8% mean reduction in Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI) ($p < 0.001$).

2. **CPAP Manufacturer Defense**: Commercial resistance from established sleep apnea hardware vendors (e.g., ResMed).
3. **Long-Term Adherence**: Monitoring 12-month+ continuation safety data from NCT06566820 extension study.
