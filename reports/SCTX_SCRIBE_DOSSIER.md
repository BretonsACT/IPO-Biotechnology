### 🎯 Core Thesis Statement
Scribe Therapeutics represents a high-upside platform entry into next-generation *in vivo* CRISPR gene editing. Co-founded by Nobel Laureate Dr. Jennifer Doudna, Scribe engineers custom CasX enzymes ("XE" platform) designed for precise **epigenetic silencing** without introducing permanent double-stranded DNA cuts. Validated by over $1.5 Billion in strategic milestone partnerships with Eli Lilly and a direct IPO equity investment from Sanofi, Scribe's lead candidate **STX-1150** (targeting *PCSK9* for long-term LDL-C lowering) is in active Phase 1 clinical testing in Australia. With $240M in cash extending runway into early 2029, Scribe provides premier exposure to genomic editing with significantly lower off-target safety risks.

## 2. Market & Industry Dynamics

- **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: $15 Billion Cardiovascular & Metabolic Gene Editing market. Over 40 million patients globally suffer from uncontrolled hypercholesterolemia and elevated Lp(a).
